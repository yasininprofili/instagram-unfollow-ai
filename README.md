# Instagram Unfollow AI: See Who Does Not Follow You Back and Unfollow at a Careful Pace

Instagram Unfollow AI is a free Chrome extension that compares your followers and following lists on Instagram, shows the accounts that do not follow you back, and can unfollow them in bulk with randomized delays. A whitelist keeps the accounts you never want to lose.

**[Install from the Chrome Web Store](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/lbanojlcfgehondfpeipfpmdfipbjlhh?utm_source=github&utm_medium=readme&utm_campaign=instagram_unfollow_ai)** | **[Full guide and FAQ](https://www.addonschrome.com/extensions/instagram-unfollow-ai-manage-unfollowers-effortlessly.html?utm_source=github&utm_medium=readme&utm_campaign=instagram_unfollow_ai)** | **[Video demo](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yNLDflIcZaM)**

This repository is the public home for Instagram Unfollow AI documentation, release notes and the issue tracker. The extension itself is distributed through the Chrome Web Store.

## Features

- Non-follower detection: scans your followers and following lists and shows every account that does not follow you back
- Bulk unfollow from that list instead of one account at a time
- Randomized delays between unfollows
- Whitelist: accounts you mark are skipped, even during a bulk run
- Adjustable speed: conservative, balanced or fast
- Runs in your browser on the Instagram website and does not ask for your Instagram password

## How to use it

1. Install Instagram Unfollow AI from the [Chrome Web Store](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/lbanojlcfgehondfpeipfpmdfipbjlhh?utm_source=github&utm_medium=readme&utm_campaign=instagram_unfollow_ai).
2. Open Instagram in Chrome on your computer while logged in and click the extension icon. It scans your followers and following lists.
3. Go through the non-followers list and whitelist the accounts you want to keep.
4. Pick a speed and start the unfollow run.

The [full guide](https://www.addonschrome.com/extensions/instagram-unfollow-ai-manage-unfollowers-effortlessly.html?utm_source=github&utm_medium=readme&utm_campaign=instagram_unfollow_ai) explains each setting.

## Avoiding action blocks

Instagram temporarily blocks accounts that unfollow too many people too quickly, and no tool can rule that out. Use the conservative speed, work in small daily batches, and stop for the day if Instagram shows a warning. Unfollows cannot be undone in bulk: you would have to follow each account again by hand, so fill the whitelist before you start.
